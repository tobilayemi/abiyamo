Early warning system

How the ANC form leads to an alert

Every entry in the ANC form is checked in two ways. Fixed clinical thresholds catch dangerous values straight away. A risk model then weighs all {{ fields|length - 1 }} scored entries together. This page shows, section by section, which entries feed which alerts.

  1. Form sectionThe health worker fills one section of the ANC form per page.
  2. ThresholdsEntries marked "Early warning" are checked against fixed clinical thresholds.
  3. Risk modelA gradient-boosted model weighs all entries together and rates the visit Low, Moderate, High or Critical.
  4. Risk reportThe report shows the risk level, each alert with the section it came from, actions and referral.
Early warningMarks the form entries that can raise an alert.
Critical alertThe visit is rated Critical, whatever the risk model says.
High alertThe visit is rated at least High, whatever the risk model says.
Warning signAlways listed on the report. The risk model sets the level.
NotedDanger sign listed on the report and weighed by the risk model.
